## Summary
A regex in ad targeting went quadratic on long keyword lists. Ad CPU sat at 97% of its limit with heavy throttling, p95
rose to 1.5s and frontend page latency breached its SLO. A small share of ad calls hit DEADLINE_EXCEEDED.

## Resolution
Scaled ad to 4 replicas, then fixed the regex.

## Lessons
- CPU at limit + `CPUThrottlingHigh` + latency on one service with no errors elsewhere = saturation.
